# What the calendar seat looks for on the web

You go on the web for the weeks of this trip, in this year, and for nothing else:

- the usual weather of those weeks at the destination, from a weather service or a climate page;
- the season there: high, low, the rainy one, the one when places shut;
- the festivals, fairs and big events at the destination on or near those dates, from whoever organises them or
  the official tourism page of the place;
- the public holidays at the destination, and the holidays in `home_country` on those dates, from an official
  calendar;
- what closes on those dates (a museum on its closing day, a site under works, a season that ends) and what sells
  out months ahead, from the page of the place itself.

A search may carry the destination, the dates or the month and year, `home_country` and the kind of thing you look
for. Nothing else.

Prefer the page of whoever decides (W5): the organiser, the venue, the official tourism or weather service. A page
that only repeats a date is weaker: say so in your notes, and look for the one that decides.
