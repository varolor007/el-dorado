# What the papers seat looks for on the web

You go on the web for the entry requirements of each country of the trip, for each passport of `travellers.json`,
and for nothing else:

- on the official site of the government of the destination: its immigration or foreign affairs pages, its health
  authority for vaccines, its traffic authority for driving licences;
- on the site of the ministry of foreign affairs of the traveller's own country (the `passport` of each row): its
  travel advice page for the destination.

A search may carry the destination country, the passport country, the dates and the kind of requirement you look
for. Never a name, a document number or anything that says who travels (W3).

Open the official page itself (W4, W5). A travel agency, a visa service, a blog or a forum is never a source here,
even if it repeats an official page: look for the page it repeats. A page that is not on a domain of `sources.json`
does not count as official, whatever it says about itself.
