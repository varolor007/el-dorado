# The tickets seat: step by step

The steps below are your steps 3 to 5 of the common method.

## 1. Read the sights

Read `in/trip.json`, `in/sights.json`, `in/open_data.json` and `in/limits.json`. Write in your plan, for each sight,
what open data already says and what you will look for: its prices for this group, its hours on the dates of the
trip, its closed days and whether it is booked ahead. A sight with an official site in open data: plan to open that
site first. Your searches and pages are shared by all the sights of this call: plan them so that none is left
without a look.

## 2. Find the sight's own page

For each sight, open its official site, or search for it by its name and the destination. The page that decides is
the sight's own (its "visit", "tickets", "opening hours" or "plan your visit" page), or the official page of whoever
runs it (a ministry, a city, a parks service). A ticket reseller, a travel agency, a blog or a review site is never a
source here: look for the page it repeats.

## 3. Write what the page says

For each sight, write a note from the page you opened, with its address and the day you saw it, for each thing it
answers: the prices and for whom, the hours and for which dates, the days it closes, whether to book and where.
Copy each number as the page gives it. When open data says one thing and the page another, the page wins: say so in
your notes. When the page does not say something, it is `unknown`.

## 4. Fit it to this trip

- Prices: the kinds of ticket that matter to this group: adults, and for each child its age against the ages the page
  gives. A sight with no ticket at all (a street, a lookout, a church that does not charge) is `free` only if a page
  says so, or says nothing is charged.
- Hours: only the opening times that hold on the dates of the trip. If the page gives seasons, write the one or ones
  that touch the trip, each with its dates. If the page gives the hours but not for which dates, `from` and `to` are
  `unknown`.
- Closed days: the days of the trip the page says it is closed, beyond its weekly hours.
- Booking: whether the page says to book ahead (a timed entry, a daily quota, a permit), and its booking address.

## 5. Self-critique

Before delivering: a sight missing or repeated; a price or an hour without a page you opened, or with no date; a
price that is a guess, a range or a conversion; a free sight without a page that says so; an opening time for other
dates; a booking address with a key, a code or a session in it; a word that promises a place or a price.
