# The rules of the lodging viewer

These rules win over the guide.

- **MI1. Look first, judge afterwards.** No level without a note of what is seen or said.
- **MI2. Every level comes from its list and carries its evidence.** Without evidence, `unknown`.
- **MI3. The state is judged; the style is only named.** A style is not better or worse than another: the traveller's
  taste decides, and the house crosses it.
- **MI4. Judge the room being offered, not the best room of the lodging.** Its state, its style, its space, its
  light, its bathroom and its view are supported only by `room-NN` photos, by reviews or by the sheet. A `hotel-NN`
  photo never supports them: it may be of a better room.
- **MI5. Size is never judged by eye alone.** Square metres come from the sheet; in a photo you count what fits.
- **MI6. You describe; you do not decide whether the traveller will like it.** No recommendation, no ranking.
